      About MAISON NOIR

      Craft, Patience, Permanence

      Brand story

      A Studio, Not a Factory

      We design in small runs, in our own studio, and work with mills that share our obsession with fibre quality. This is a demonstration site: the brand, products and prices are fictional.

      Mission

      What We Believe

      Fewer, better

      Small collections, drafted carefully, designed to replace the impulse with the keeper.

      Honest materials

      Natural fibres, transparent sourcing notes and construction built to be repaired.

      Made to be altered

      Hems, seams and linings designed so that a garment can change shape with you.

      Support

      Frequently Asked

      How do sizes fit?

      Tailored pieces run true to size with a structured shoulder. Knitwear is cut slightly relaxed. Check the size guide on each product.

      What is your returns policy?

      This demo describes a 14-day return window for unworn pieces. A live store would show its full terms here.

      Do you offer alterations?

      Yes, in the live concept, hems and minor tailoring are offered through our studio. This is a demo, so no service is booked.

      Is my payment real on this site?

      No. Checkout is a front-end demonstration. No payment is taken and no order is placed.
